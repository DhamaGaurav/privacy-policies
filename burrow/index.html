Burrow Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026

Your data stays on your device
01 — What data exists 02 — Where it lives 03 — Documents & camera 04 — Reminders 05 — Backup & restore 06 — Purchases 07 — Analytics & tracking 08 — Children 09 — Changes 10 — Contact

Burrow keeps track of your warranties and insurance, and tells you before they lapse. This policy is short because the answer to almost every privacy question is the same: it stays on your phone.

§

Burrow has no server, no account system, and no login. Nobody at Burrow, including the developer, can see your items, policies, policy numbers or documents. There is nothing to see: it never leaves your device unless you personally export and share it.

01

What data exists

The app stores what you put into it. None of it is collected by Burrow; it is created by you, for you, and used only to show you what is covered and when cover ends.

Items
Things you own (appliances, phones, vehicles, shop equipment and so on) with purchase details you choose to add
Cover
Warranties, extended warranties, service contracts and insurance policies: provider, dates, premium, sum insured, policy number, notes
People and spaces
Names of family members or a business that you group your items under
Documents
Photos, scans and files you attach, such as receipts and policy papers
Settings
Reminder timing, theme and app-lock choice, stored on-device only
02

Where it lives

Everything is stored on-device, using Apple's SwiftData framework on iPhone and a local Room database on Android. There is no Burrow server, no cloud sync, and no backend anywhere. If you delete the app, your data goes with it, unless you have exported a backup first.

03

Documents & camera

When you scan, photograph or import a document, Burrow asks your phone for camera or file access at that moment. The result is saved inside the app on your device. Burrow does not upload it, and does not read the contents for any purpose other than showing it back to you.

04

Reminders

Reminders are notifications scheduled by your phone itself. They are created and delivered on-device from dates you entered. Burrow sends nothing to a notification server.

05

Backup & restore

Burrow lets you export everything to a single backup file, entirely at your own request, and import it back later, for example on a new phone. That file is yours from the moment it is created. Burrow has no visibility into where you save it, who you send it to, or what becomes of it afterward. Keep it somewhere you trust, because it contains your policy details.

06

Purchases

Burrow is free to use, and may offer an optional paid upgrade. If it does, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. Burrow never sees your payment details.

07

Analytics & tracking

None. No analytics SDK, no crash reporting service, no advertising network, no third party of any kind is integrated into Burrow. Nothing about your usage is measured, and nothing is shared, sold, or used for tracking across apps or websites.

08

Children's privacy

Burrow isn't directed at children, but since nothing is collected from anyone, of any age, there is no children's data to protect beyond what already applies to every user above.

09

Changes to this policy

If Burrow's data practices ever change, for example an optional cloud-sync or household-sharing feature is added later, this page will be updated first, with a new effective date above, before that feature ships.

10

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be sent to the developer, Dhama Works, through the contact details on Burrow's App Store or Google Play listing.
