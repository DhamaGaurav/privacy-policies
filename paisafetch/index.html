PaisaFetch Privacy Policy

Effective September 2026

Ledger stays on your device
01 — What data exists 02 — Where it lives 03 — Message parsing 04 — Notifications 05 — Exporting data 06 — Purchases 07 — Analytics & tracking 08 — Children 09 — Changes 10 — Contact

PaisaFetch keeps a ledger of your expenses, income, and money habits. This policy is short because the answer to almost every privacy question is the same: it stays on your phone.

§

PaisaFetch has no server, no account system, and no login. Nobody at PaisaFetch — including the developer — can see your transactions, your balances, or your messages. There is nothing to see: it never leaves your device unless you personally export and share it.

01

What data exists

The app stores what you type or import into it: expense and income entries, categories, banks, budgets, Kitty (split-expense) groups, Commitments entries, and the regex rules you write to parse bank messages. None of it is collected by PaisaFetch — it's created by you, for you.

Financial records
Amounts, dates, categories, notes, banks, payment modes
Group data
Split-expense participants and settlements you name yourself
Automation rules
The message-parsing patterns you author, stored locally
02

Where it lives

Everything is stored on-device using Apple's own SwiftData framework — the same private storage every well-behaved iOS app uses for its own files. There is no PaisaFetch database, no cloud sync, and no backend server anywhere. If you delete the app, the ledger goes with it.

03

Message parsing

PaisaFetch can turn a bank SMS or notification into a transaction automatically — but only through a Shortcuts automation that you build and run on your own device. PaisaFetch never reads your Messages app, never runs in the background scanning notifications, and never receives message content unless your own Shortcut hands it over, on your phone, for that one instant. That text is parsed on-device and discarded once the transaction is logged.

04

Notifications

Spend-threshold alerts and trip-end reminders are local notifications, scheduled and delivered entirely on your device via Apple's own notification framework. Nothing is sent to a push server to trigger them, because there isn't one.

05

Exporting your data

Settings → Manage Data lets you export everything to a JSON file, entirely at your own request. That file is yours from the moment it's created — PaisaFetch has no visibility into where you save it, who you send it to, or what becomes of it afterward. Importing works the same way, in reverse.

06

Purchases

PaisaFetch Pro, where offered, is handled entirely by Apple's StoreKit and your App Store account. PaisaFetch never sees or stores your payment details — only a yes/no record of whether the purchase was made, checked against Apple's own systems.

07

Analytics & tracking

None. No analytics SDK, no crash reporting service, no advertising network, no third party of any kind is integrated into PaisaFetch. Nothing about your usage is measured, and nothing is shared, sold, or used for tracking across apps or websites.

08

Children's privacy

PaisaFetch isn't directed at children, but the point is somewhat moot: since nothing is collected from anyone, of any age, there is no children's data to protect beyond what already applies to every user above.

09

Changes to this policy

If PaisaFetch's data practices ever change — say, an optional cloud-sync feature is added later — this page will be updated first, with a new effective date above, before that feature ships.
